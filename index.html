The BDI draft grader

Every drafted roster is measured on four things and then ranked against the whole field: value relative to where the pick was made (35%), discipline against consensus rank (25%), roster construction against the league's own starting slots (25%), and projected starting lineup strength (15%). The method was frozen before either draft began.
